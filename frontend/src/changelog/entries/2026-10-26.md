title: Feedback and what's new
---

- **What's new** — this dialog. It opens by itself whenever TripMap has
  changed since your last visit, and you can reopen it any time from the
  navigation bar.
- **Feedback** — found a bug or missing something? Where your server has
  feedback enabled, the speech-bubble button in the navigation bar sends a
  message, with a screenshot if you like.
- **Immich albums** — link an album to an Immich album to browse its photos
  and pick a cover.
